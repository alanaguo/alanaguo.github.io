About

I am a fourth-year PhD candidate in the Department of Computer Science at Example University, advised by Prof. Jane Smith. My research is in natural language processing and machine learning, with a focus on making language models reliable when data is scarce or the test distribution shifts.

Before my PhD, I received a B.S. in Computer Science from Sample University in 2022.
